# Fischl — Drawing Studio 4.0 fan-art

An original anime-inspired raster fan-art scene built directly on the Drawing Studio canvas. The artwork is generated locally by `drawing-engine/fischl-art.js` into editable native raster layers. It is not an SVG, not an imported flattened illustration, and does not call a paid or external image-generation service.

## Open

https://azartworld55-ctrl.github.io/Drawing-Studio/#fischl

The `#fischl` route starts the artwork automatically. On the regular studio page, use **⚡ Нарисовать Фишль**. Save an editable layered `.dsp` project or export a flattened PNG using the studio controls.

## Art direction

- Character: Fischl, the Prinzessin der Verurteilung; a theatrical young adventurer associated with Electro and her raven familiar Oz.
- Composition: expressive three-quarter portrait with a raised hand, long flowing blonde hair, eyepatch, gothic-inspired purple outfit, and Oz hovering at her shoulder.
- Palette: midnight indigo, violet, antique gold, warm ivory, pale blonde, and restrained lavender Electro accents.
- Lighting: cool moonlight in the environment, warmer soft key light on the face.
- Setting: moonlit Mondstadt-inspired rooftops, distant stars and restrained electro ribbons.

This is an original interpretation of the character, not a copy or trace of official key art.

## Drawing Studio 4.0 workflow

1. Native raster layers separate background, atmosphere, body/clothing, hair/headpiece/face, Oz, costume details, finishing, and brush accents.
2. Reference Workspace receives a generated design-study sheet showing the palette, silhouette notes and light direction. It stays separate from the artwork and is not exported.
3. Perspective Assistant guides are enabled during environment construction and hidden for the finished view.
4. Pose & Anatomy Lab creates a separate hidden dynamic-pose construction layer.
5. Color & Lighting Lab is set to analogous harmony and upper-left key light.
6. Grayscale preview is toggled for a tonal pass and returned to color.
7. Art Review runs technical canvas analysis; subjective checklist items remain for human review.
8. Expressive accents use the local MyPaint WASM adapter when available, with a local raster brush fallback.
